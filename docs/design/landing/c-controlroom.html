Why it exists

An agent with hands is only useful if someone can still reach the brake.

Most agent tooling gives you a sandbox and a transcript. Summrise gives the agent the actual box — and gives you the console, the grants and the audit that make handing it over a decision rather than a leap of faith.

What changes

From "the AI said it ran it" to a row you can read.

01 / SESSIONS

The machine, not a mock

Terminal, SSH, serial and Chrome, on the device's own network. Attach, resize, scroll back — the same shell a person would use.

02 / CONTROL

Approve, hold, revoke

Destructive tools are refused unless the token allows them. Hold the keyboard and the agent waits. Revoke a grant and the next call is not authorised.

03 / PROOF

Audit by construction

Every call is a signed grant and an audit row: who asked, what ran, what came back. No reconstruction from logs after the fact.
